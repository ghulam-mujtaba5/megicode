# Megicode Pricing Implementation Guide

## Commercial Position

Megicode should sell as a founder-friendly AI product engineering partner: clearer and more premium than low-cost freelance pricing, but still below mature Western product studios. The public pricing system should use USD, fixed-scope packages, and "from" pricing where scope varies.

Use this public message:

**Clear starting prices for AI automation, SaaS MVPs, and custom business platforms. Start small, validate the scope, then scale when the product or workflow is clear.**

Avoid language like cheap, low-cost, best price, or hourly developers. It weakens trust for international buyers.

## Website Journey

The desired buyer flow is:

Homepage -> Pricing preview -> Pricing page -> Service page or Contact -> Proposal

For uncertain founders:

Homepage -> MVP Roadmap -> Contact -> Roadmap purchase

For clinic buyers:

Clinic package -> Contact form -> Fit call

For larger builds:

Pricing -> Roadmap or Free Fit Call -> Proposal

## Page Requirements

### Pricing Page

Use this order:

1. Hero with clear USD pricing message.
2. Five starting-point cards.
3. Package tabs: Roadmap, Automation, Clinic, SaaS MVP, Platforms, Support.
4. Pricing cards with price, best-for line, timeline, inclusions, note, and CTA.
5. Cost drivers section.
6. Proof strip.
7. FAQ.
8. Final fit-call CTA.

The pricing page should reduce fear. Do not overload the first screen with a huge table.

### Homepage

Show a compact pricing preview after the services section:

- MVP Roadmap - from $400
- AI Automation - from $900
- Clinic AI Receptionist - from $1,250 setup
- AI SaaS MVP - from $4,500
- Custom Platform - from $3,500

Primary CTA: **View Pricing**

Secondary CTA: **Book Free Fit Call**

### Services Page

Add a productized "ways to work" section with six cards:

- MVP Roadmap
- AI Automation Setup
- Clinic AI Receptionist
- AI SaaS MVP Build
- Custom Business Platform
- Monthly Support / Retainer

Each card should show a starting price, timeline, best-for line, and CTA.

### Contact Page

The form should qualify leads without rejecting low-budget users. Required fields can stay minimal, but the form should ask for:

- Company
- Country
- Service interested in
- Budget range
- Timeline
- Project stage
- Designs or requirements status
- NDA need
- Preferred meeting time zone

Low budgets should route toward Roadmap or smaller automation work rather than being blocked.

## UI Direction

Use Megicode's existing brand system:

- Primary blue: `#4573df`
- Deep blue: `#2d4fa2`
- Action orange: `#ff9800`
- Light surfaces: `#ffffff`, `#f8fafc`, `#e8eaf6`
- Dark surface: `#1d2127`

Pricing should feel stable and serious. Use calm card lifts, tabs, accordions, and proof strips. Avoid heavy animation, noisy dashboards, generic robot graphics, or too many badges.

Use orange for the most important action only: fit-call CTAs, Most Popular badges, and key pricing highlights.

## Pricing Rules

Use rounded B2B prices:

- Correct: `$400`, `$900`, `$1,800`, `$4,500`
- Avoid: `$397`, `$899`, `$4,497`

Use "from" pricing for complex work:

- Correct: **AI SaaS MVP Build - from $4,500**
- Wrong: **AI SaaS MVP - $4,500 only**

Use setup plus monthly pricing for clinic automation:

**$1,250 setup + $150/month**

This sets the expectation that clinic automation needs monitoring and support.
